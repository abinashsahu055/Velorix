SEO services

Everything your small business needs to be found online, from technical fixes to AI search.

Technical SEO

Fix the site issues that stop search engines from crawling and understanding your pages.

  • Crawlability and indexing review
  • Site speed and Core Web Vitals checks
  • Mobile usability and HTTPS review
  • XML sitemap, robots.txt and canonical tags
  • Structured data (schema) setup

On-page SEO

Make every important page clear to both searchers and search engines.

  • Title tags and meta descriptions
  • Heading structure and internal links
  • Image alt text and URL cleanup
  • Search intent matching for each page

Off-page SEO

Build your reputation across the web with honest, relevant signals.

  • Backlink profile review
  • Relevant, quality link opportunities
  • Brand mentions and citations
  • No paid link schemes or spam tactics

Local SEO

Help nearby customers find your business in map results and local searches.

  • Google Business Profile optimization
  • Consistent name, address and phone details
  • Local landing pages
  • Review request process and reply guidance

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)

Structure your content so it can be used as a direct answer to common questions.

  • Question-based content planning
  • Clear, concise answer sections and FAQs
  • FAQ and how-to schema where it fits
  • Featured snippet and voice search focus

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)

Make your brand easier for AI search tools and chat assistants to understand and reference.

  • Clear entity and brand information
  • Well-organized, trustworthy source content
  • Consistent facts across your website and profiles
  • Tracking mentions in AI answers (no guarantees)

Keyword research

Find the searches your future customers actually make.

  • Customer and competitor research
  • Search intent grouping
  • Priority list by opportunity and effort
  • Keyword-to-page mapping

Content optimization

Improve existing pages and plan new ones that answer real questions.

  • Content audit of current pages
  • Rewrite and expansion guidance
  • Content gaps and new page ideas
  • Readable, helpful writing for people first
